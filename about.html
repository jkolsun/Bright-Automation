Our story

From a Penn State friendship to a platform.

Where it started Pennsylvania State University, where co-founders Jared Kolsun and Andrew Tesauro met as neighbors during the summer session before their freshman year.
01

A shared idea at Penn State

Bright Automations was born from a friendship forged at Pennsylvania State University. As students pursuing technology and business, Jared and Andrew shared one vision: make a professional web presence accessible to every business owner.

02

The problem we kept seeing

Too many businesses (plumbers, roofers, lawyers, you name it) were losing jobs because they didn't have a decent website. Or they were paying $3,000+ for one. We knew we could do it better: professional sites, built fast, at a price that actually makes sense.

03

From a website to a system

A website alone doesn't make the phone ring, so we never hand one over and disappear. Every plan includes hosting, connecting your site to your Google Business Profile, monthly AI and SEO updates and a performance report every month. For businesses ready for more, the Client Command Center answers every new lead in seconds.

04

Built to scale

We built our own software to design a custom preview for a business before they pay anything. It has produced more than 1,000,000 website previews for local businesses in all 50 states. More than 2,000 custom websites have been built for individual businesses.

FAQ

Bright Automations, in plain answers.

Who founded Bright Automations?

Bright Automations was founded by Jared Kolsun, Co-Founder and CTO, and Andrew Tesauro, Co-Founder and CEO. They met as neighbors during Penn State's summer session before their freshman year, later joined the same fraternity, and started the company in September 2025 going into their junior year. They still lead it today.

How many businesses use Bright Automations?

Bright Automations has built more than 2,000 custom websites and generated more than 1,000,000 custom website previews for local businesses in all 50 states, across 30+ industries.

What does Bright Automations do?

Bright Automations builds and runs websites for local service businesses as a monthly service. The plan includes a custom website, hosting and SSL, connecting the site to your Google Business Profile once it's live, speed and mobile optimization, monthly AI and SEO updates and monthly performance reports. Businesses can add AI systems such as instant lead response and missed-call text-back, and the Client Command Center package with The Bright Center app.

How much does Bright Automations cost?

The website plan is $99.95 per month with $0 setup, and you see a free preview of your site before you pay. The Client Command Center, our Google Local Services Ads package with The Bright Center app, is $399 per month plus ad spend paid to Google, and SEO Premier, full Google Business Profile management, is a $45.95 per month add-on.
