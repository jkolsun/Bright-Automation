When an agency is the better pick

If you run several locations, spend a lot on ads, or want regular strategy meetings with a team, a good agency can be worth its retainer. We work by text, with no calls or meetings needed, and we don't sell a strategy team. If that's what you want, an agency is the honest answer.

When we're the better pick

If you're a one or two truck business and $500 to $3,000 a month is hard to justify, we built this for you. You see your actual site before you pay a dime, there's no setup fee, and you can cancel anytime. Fill out the form on the site and we build a free preview and text you the link. After that, everything happens by text, with no calls or meetings needed. We've designed 2,000+ custom websites and hold a 5.0 rating from 70+ Google reviews.

If money gets tight, cut waste, not customers

A lot of economists are worried about 2027. Nobody knows for sure, but owners are already asking what to cut. Harvard Business Review studied 4,700 public companies across three past recessions, and the group most likely to come out ahead cut costs through efficiency while still investing more than their rivals in marketing (Gulati, Nohria and Wohlgezogen, HBR 2010). For a local business, I read that simply: keep the things that make the phone ring, and cut the spend you can't connect to a call. Ask whoever you pay one question: what did last month's spend bring in? If they can't answer, that's the line to cut.

Questions owners ask us

Is a marketing agency worth it for a small business?

It can be, when you can see what it brings in. Published local SEO retainers run $500 to $3,000 a month (WebFX), so ask for a report that shows calls and booked jobs, not just traffic. If the retainer clearly pays for itself, keep it. If nobody can tell you what last month's spend produced, that's your answer.

What is a cheaper alternative to a marketing agency?

Start with the free basics: keep your Google Business Profile complete and active, ask every customer for a review, and make sure your site loads fast on a phone with your number tappable. Then, if you want it handled, a done-for-you monthly plan costs far less than a typical retainer. Ours is $99.95 a month for the website with $0 setup, and SEO Premier is $45.95 a month for full Google Business Profile management.

How much should a small business pay for SEO?

Published ranges put a local SEO retainer at $500 to $3,000 a month (WebFX), and the most common SEO agency retainer at $500 to $1,000 a month (Ahrefs). Our SEO Premier is $45.95 a month. Whatever you pay, get a monthly report and a clear list of what's done.

Should I cut marketing if money gets tight?

Cut waste, not the things that bring in calls. In Harvard Business Review's study of three past recessions, the companies most likely to pull ahead cut costs through efficiency and kept investing in marketing. The smart cut is the spend nobody can connect to a customer.

Do I have to sign a contract with Bright Automations?

No long-term contract and no setup fee. We build your site first, you see it free, and you pay only once you want it live. You can cancel anytime, and you own your domain and content.
