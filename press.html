Full

Bright Automations is an AI-powered marketing and growth company serving local service businesses across the United States. Jared Kolsun and Andrew Tesauro, who met at Penn State, founded it in September 2025. The company builds each business a custom website before they pay, with $0 setup and $99.95 a month. Its SEO Premier service manages clients' Google Business Profiles, and its Client Command Center package runs Google Local Services Ads alongside The Bright Center, the company's app for answering leads, texting back missed calls and requesting reviews. Every client gets a monthly report on calls, traffic and next steps. Bright Automations has designed more than 2,000 custom websites and holds a 5.0 rating across 70+ Google reviews.

Jared Kolsun is co-founder and CTO of Bright Automations, which builds AI-powered marketing and growth systems for local service businesses across the United States. From Conshohocken, Pennsylvania, he is a double major in Cybersecurity Analytics and Operations and Enterprise Technology Integration at Penn State, with a focus on artificial intelligence. He leads the technology behind the company, including The Bright Center app, and oversees every client build. He co-founded Bright Automations in September 2025 with Andrew Tesauro, whom he met as neighbors during Penn State's summer session in 2023.
