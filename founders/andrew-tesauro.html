At Bright Automations

What Andrew owns.

Andrew met Jared Kolsun as neighbors during Penn State's summer session in 2023, right before their freshman year, and they later joined the same fraternity. They started Bright Automations in September 2025, going into their junior year, by cold-calling businesses and walking into shops near campus. It has since designed more than 2,000 custom websites.

  1. Sales

    Andrew leads sales: walking business owners through their free custom preview and the plan behind it.

  2. The first conversation

    An owner fills out the form, we build a free preview and text them the link. Everything after that happens by text, with no calls or meetings needed.

  3. The right fit

    Making sure every client gets exactly what they need, from the first preview through launch day.

  4. Growth

    Growth and client relationships across 30+ industries, from roofing and HVAC to landscaping, cleaning and auto detailing.

  5. Giving back

    Andrew's family runs the Helping Hands Foundation, which helps people with cancer pay for treatment.
