At Bright Automations

What Jared owns.

Jared met Andrew Tesauro as neighbors during Penn State's summer session before their freshman year. They later joined the same fraternity and started Bright Automations in September 2025, going into their junior year. The company has built more than 2,000 custom websites for local businesses.

  1. The build

    Every Bright Automations site is designed and built under Jared's lead, from the free preview to launch day. His standard: clean design that actually converts visitors into calls.

  2. The platform

    Jared leads the technology behind Bright Automations, including the preview software that has generated more than 1,000,000 custom website previews for local businesses in all 50 states.

  3. Hosting and performance

    Hosting, SSL, speed and mobile optimization for every live site, so business owners never have to think about it.

  4. Monthly improvements

    The monthly AI and SEO updates that keep every client's site current long after launch.
